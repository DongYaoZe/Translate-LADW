# Translation provenance

This repository contains Dong Yaoze's Chinese translation and typesetting of Sergei Treil's *Linear Algebra Done Wrong*.

## Authoritative English source

The current revision is checked against Sergei Treil's official **2026-04-30** version, published on the author's website through LaTeXML-generated HTML.

During maintenance, the official webpages may be converted into local structured working files to make chapter-, theorem-, formula-, and exercise-level comparison easier. These working files are editorial aids only and are **not** represented as the author's original LaTeX source.

## Chinese translation provenance

Chinese prose in this repository comes from:

1. Dong Yaoze's existing Chinese translation, retained and revised where appropriate;
2. fresh translation or revision made directly against the official English version;
3. clearly identified translator notes and editorial guidance written for this project.

The aim is to preserve the translator's own Chinese expression while improving mathematical accuracy, fluency, terminology consistency, cross-references, and maintainability.

## Figures and structure

Figures introduced during the 2026 revision come from assets published with the author's official 2026 webpages. Chapter organization, theorem families, formulas, and exercises are checked against the official version while keeping the Chinese edition's own typesetting and translator material.

## Release checks

Before a public release, the project is checked for structural consistency, exercise/answer completeness, references, and successful PDF compilation. The resulting PDF is also visually reviewed at key pages before publication.
